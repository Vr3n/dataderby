a season.

Where. The #stages select, on the show and team-statistics pages.

Only when needed. A single-stage season renders no select.

Not in the feed. No stage name in the JSON, and no name in the page config.

Order lies. CL lists Final Stage first; the Championship lists the league first.

Final Stage ≠ final. It is the knockout phase, all rounds.

2022/23 CL had nine stage ids: eight groups plus the knockout. Identity is the id, never the label.

Without the header the feed returns HTML, not JSON.

Headless is blocked. Cloudflare serves the challenge page.

The anomaly

Conference League stage 25802 does not look like the league phase. It may be a qualifier, or the feed may be mid-publication. Verify before the first scrape.

Knockout

Team statistics exist, but there is no league table. has_league_table is a per-stage attribute, not a season default.

Format check

Domestic leagues have 18–24 teams. The UEFA league phase has 36. The stage, not the competition name, decides the shape.

So what

The feed is uniform. The season format is not. Stage discovery belongs in the pipeline, not in a hardcoded map.

All twelve return the same three sections; only the row count and the stage shape differ.

Recommendation

Ship A + C.
Defer the rest.

1. Build the 60-column fact over the stage axis.

2. Add the standings fact at the same (snapshot, team) grain.

3. Persist the page, the three standings views and the nine feeds.

4. Licence accepted 20 Oct; keep it local, private, non-redistributed.

Not in v1

Overall-only. Situational grids. xG panel. Player and referee stats.

Fail closed

Unknown stage label, missing token, a team count that does not match the format, or standings ids that do not match the feed: stop the run, keep the evidence.
